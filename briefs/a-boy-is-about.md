# Rubber Milo

- **Reel folder:** `reels/a-boy-is-about/`
- **Length:** 45 s · 1080×1920 · 30 fps
- **Look:** soft 3D low-poly toy diorama. Base: library style *Low-poly Isometric Island* (`~/lemo-opuscar/styles/lowpoly-island/STYLE.md`): flat-shaded pastel facets, orthographic camera, no outlines, no black, drop-and-squash landings, pop-in props. Diorama sits on a floating street tile in a pastel gradient sky.
- **VO file:** `audio/a-boy-is-about-vo.wav` (lines below are suggestions; the cut is timed to the recording)
- **Safe zones:** key action and text stay out of the bottom 20 % and right 12 % (platform UI)

## Logline
A brick falls out of a cloud straight onto a little boy, and it turns out he's made of rubber.

## Hook (0–3 s)
Cold open, no title. Low close-up: Milo licking an ice cream on a toy street. A dark square shadow blooms around his feet and grows. He looks up. The brick tumbles into the top of the frame, filling a third of it. Freeze for 6 frames on a red "!" pop above his head. The viewer has to know whether he's going to be OK.

## Cast & world
- **Milo**: small boy, round head, orange tee, rubbery yellow-peach skin; must squash flat, stretch tall, wobble (`bucket-shared/rig-rubberkid.js`, NEW)
- **Brick**: chunky terracotta brick, slightly bevelled (`bucket-shared/prop-brick.js`, NEW)
- **Toy town**: one floating street tile: sidewalk, lamp post, mailbox, two box houses with prism roofs, cone trees, one fluffy faceted cloud (`bucket-shared/world-toytown.js`, NEW)
- **Pip the dog**: tiny low-poly dog who reacts (part of `world-toytown.js` as a prop)
